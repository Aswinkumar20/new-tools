How to use Pathology Slide Viewer
  • Upload PNG/JPEG/WebP pathology slide images. SVS/TIFF may warn but can still decode if the browser supports them.
  • Use stain presets (H&E default, bright, nuclear, eosin) or adjust brightness/contrast manually.
  • Switch annotation mode: Navigate (pan when zoomed), Point (click), or Rectangle (drag). List and delete marks in the sidebar.
  • Export original, annotations JSON, summary JSON, or a PNG snapshot. Education/research only — not for diagnostic use.
