How to use NRRD Viewer
  • Upload .nrrd or detached .nhdr headers. Raw and gzip encodings are supported for common scalar types.
  • Switch axial, coronal, or sagittal planes and scroll slices with the slider, arrow keys, or mouse wheel. Window/level and grayscale or hot colormap adjust display.
  • Inspect the intensity histogram in the sidebar. Export original, header JSON, summary JSON, or a PNG of the current slice. Files never leave your browser.
  • Education disclaimer: this tool is for learning and research workflows only — not for diagnostic or clinical use.
