How to use PET Scan Viewer
  • Upload uncompressed PT .dcm / .dicom / .ima. Non-PT modalities soft-warn but still load.
  • Hot colormap is default; toggle grayscale with the toolbar or C. Click to probe rescaled SUV-like values.
  • Load CT alongside PT to enable fuse overlay — anatomy grayscale plus hot PET with adjustable opacity.
  • Wheel or arrow keys navigate slices. Export original, metadata JSON, summary JSON, or PNG snapshot.
  • Education/research only — not for diagnosis. Files never leave your browser.
