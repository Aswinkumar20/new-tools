How to use MINC Viewer
  • Upload .mnc or .minc NetCDF classic (MINC 1) volumes. MINC 2 / HDF5 is not supported — convert first.
  • Switch axial, coronal, or sagittal planes and scroll slices with the slider, arrow keys, or mouse wheel. Window/level and grayscale or hot colormap adjust display.
  • Inspect NetCDF metadata in the sidebar table — variable name, dimensions, and data range.
  • Export original, header JSON, summary JSON, or a PNG of the current slice. Files never leave your browser.
