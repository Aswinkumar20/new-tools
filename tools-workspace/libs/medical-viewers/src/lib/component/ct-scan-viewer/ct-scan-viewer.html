How to use CT Scan Viewer
  • Upload uncompressed CT .dcm / .dicom / .ima. Other modalities warn softly but still load.
  • Wheel / trackpad scrolls slices; arrow keys and the slice slider also navigate the series.
  • CT HU presets (Soft Tissue, Lung, Bone, Abdomen, Brain). Click to probe HU; Measure for px/mm distance when PixelSpacing is present.
  • Export original, metadata JSON, summary JSON, or a PNG snapshot. Not for diagnostic use.
