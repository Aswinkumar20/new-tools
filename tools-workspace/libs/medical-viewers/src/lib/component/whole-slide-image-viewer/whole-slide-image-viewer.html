How to use Whole Slide Image Viewer
  • Upload PNG/JPEG/WebP/BMP/TIF exports or rendered whole-slide images. Native SVS pyramids warn softly.
  • Use pyramid level controls and zoom to navigate large slides. The minimap shows your viewport rectangle.
  • Draw rectangular regions of interest with the Region tool. List and export them as JSON from the sidebar or toolbar.
  • Export original, regions JSON, summary JSON, or a PNG snapshot. Education/research only — not for diagnostic use.
