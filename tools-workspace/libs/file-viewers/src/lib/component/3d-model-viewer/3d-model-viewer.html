Roadmap

Drag-and-drop uploads, validation, and metadata extraction are on the roadmap.

  • Initial support for GLTF/GLB, OBJ, STL, and FBX models.
  • Environment maps and HDR lighting presets.
  • Section cutting, measurements, and annotations.

Models will be processed locally in your browser.

How to use
  • Upload GLTF, GLB, OBJ, STL, or FBX files once the viewer launches.
  • Orbit, pan, and zoom around models with mouse and touch controls.
  • Inspect mesh metadata, materials, and bounding dimensions in the sidebar.
