About PowerPoint Viewer

Everything you need to review slide decks without leaving the browser.

What is PowerPoint Viewer?

PowerPoint Viewer processes presentations in your browser, keeping private decks on your device. Review slides, zoom into details, and navigate multi-slide decks with ease.

How to use

  1. Upload: Drag and drop PPTX, PPT, or ODP files or use the "Select Presentation Files" button.
  2. Navigate: Move between slides with arrow buttons, keyboard arrows, or by entering a slide number.
  3. Zoom: Adjust zoom levels between 50% and 200% to match your viewing needs.
  4. Fullscreen: Toggle fullscreen for focused reviews or live presentations.
  5. Download/Print: Export the current slide for sharing or offline reference.

Key features

  • Multiple format support: Works with PPTX, PPT, and ODP files.
  • Precise slide navigation: Keyboard-ready controls with slide jumping.
  • Rich zoom options: View slides in detail or fit them to width.
  • Drag & drop uploads: Add decks directly from your desktop.
  • Privacy-first: All processing happens locally in your browser.

Supported formats

Presentation formats: PPTX, PPT, ODP

File size limit: 100MB per file

Browser compatibility: Works on all modern browsers (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge)

Limitations & notes

  • Complex animations or embedded media may not render perfectly in the browser.
  • PPT and ODP decks are supported, but converting to PPTX yields the best results.
  • Password-protected presentations are not currently supported.
  • Very large decks may take longer to process depending on your device.
