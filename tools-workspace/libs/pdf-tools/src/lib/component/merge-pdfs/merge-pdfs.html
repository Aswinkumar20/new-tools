How to Use

Step-by-step guide to merge your PDF documents

1

Upload PDF Files

Click the "Browse" button or drag and drop multiple PDF files into the upload area. You can upload as many PDF files as you need. Each file can be up to 100MB in size.

2

Reorder Files (Optional)

If you want to change the order of PDFs in the merged document, click and drag the files in the list to reorder them. The files will be merged in the order shown in the list.

3

Configure Options

Choose your merge options:

  • Preserve bookmarks: Keep bookmarks from the source PDFs in the merged document.
  • Remove duplicate pages: Automatically remove pages that appear multiple times across the PDFs.
  • Output filename: Enter a custom name for your merged PDF file (optional).
4

Merge & Download

Click the "Merge PDFs" button to combine all the uploaded files. Once the merge is complete, your browser will automatically download the merged PDF file. You can also preview the merged document before downloading.

💡 Tips & Tricks

  • You can add more PDFs at any time before merging by clicking "Add More".
  • Remove individual files by clicking the X button next to any file in the list.
  • Use the drag handle (⋮⋮) to reorder files - the merged PDF will follow this order.
  • All processing happens in your browser - your files never leave your device.
  • Large PDFs may take a few moments to process - please be patient.
