How to Use

Step-by-step guide to add signatures to your PDF documents

1

Upload PDF

Click the "Browse" button or drag and drop your PDF file into the upload area. Supported file size: up to 50MB.

2

Create Your Signature

Choose one of three methods to create your signature:

  • Draw: Use your mouse or touchpad to draw your signature on the canvas. Use the undo button to remove the last stroke.
  • Type: Enter your name and choose from various cursive fonts. Adjust the font size to your preference.
  • Upload: Upload an image file (PNG, JPG) containing your signature.

Click "Save Signature" after creating it.

3

Place Your Signature

Click the "Place/Reposition Signature" button, then click anywhere on the PDF preview where you want to place your signature. The signature will be centered at the click location.

4

Reposition & Resize

After placing your signature, you can:

  • Drag: Click and drag the signature to move it to a different position on the same page.
  • Resize: Use the size controls to increase or decrease the signature size.
  • Change Page: Select a different page from the dropdown to place the signature on another page.
5

Navigate Pages

Use the page navigation controls (previous/next buttons or page number input) to view different pages of your PDF. Navigate to the page where you want to place your signature.

6

Download Signed PDF

Once you're satisfied with the signature placement, click "Download Signed PDF" to save your document with the signature embedded. The original PDF remains unchanged.

💡 Tips & Tricks

  • You can place multiple signatures on different pages of the same PDF.
  • The signature position is saved per page - navigate to different pages to place signatures on each.
  • Use the undo button while drawing to correct mistakes without clearing the entire signature.
  • For best results, use a white background when creating your signature image.
  • The PDF preview automatically adjusts to fit the available width while maintaining aspect ratio.
