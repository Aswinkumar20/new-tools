How it works

We scan your copy, track seen words, and flag repeat occurrences so you can tighten phrasing fast.

  • Highlights ignore casing, so “Team” and “team” count as duplicates.
  • Output preserves the first appearance of every word in order.
  • Copy the cleaned text or keep editing until duplicates drop to zero.

How to use

Remove duplicate lines from your text in just a few simple steps.

  1. Enter your text

    Paste or type your content in the source text panel. Duplicates are detected instantly.

  2. View highlights

    See duplicate words highlighted in the output panel for easy identification.

  3. Review cleaned output

    Check the cleaned text below highlights, with duplicates automatically removed.

  4. Copy results

    Click "Copy cleaned" or "Copy output" to use the deduplicated text elsewhere.

  5. Monitor statistics

    Track word count, duplicate count, and length metrics in the toolbar.
