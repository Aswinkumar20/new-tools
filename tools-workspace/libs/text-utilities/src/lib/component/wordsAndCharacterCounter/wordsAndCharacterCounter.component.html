How to use

Get started with our text analysis tool in just a few simple steps.

  1. Enter your text

    Paste or type your content into the text area. The analysis updates automatically as you type.

  2. View live statistics

    Watch real-time metrics in the toolbar: word count, characters, sentences, and paragraphs.

  3. Explore insights

    Check the sidebar for word frequency analysis and readability scores to improve your writing.

  4. Export or copy

    Use the toolbar buttons to copy text, export statistics, or download a PDF report.

  5. Use history

    Press Ctrl+Z (or Cmd+Z on Mac) to undo, or Ctrl+Y to redo changes.

About this tool

Everything you need to know about our comprehensive text analysis platform.

The Word & Character Counter is a powerful text analysis tool designed for writers, editors, marketers, and content creators. It provides real-time insights into your writing to help you create better content.

Real-time analysis

Metrics update instantly as you type, giving you immediate feedback on your content.

Word frequency

Identify overused words and improve variety in your writing with visual word clouds.

Readability scores

Multiple algorithms (Flesch, Gunning Fog, SMOG, Coleman-Liau) assess text complexity.

Export options

Download detailed PDF reports or copy statistics for sharing and documentation.
