How to use

Get started with Base64 encoding and decoding in just a few steps.

  1. Choose your mode

    Select "Encode text" to convert plain text to Base64, or "Decode Base64" to restore original content.

  2. Enter your content

    Paste or type your text in the input panel. For large files, use the "Upload file" button.

  3. View results

    See the converted output instantly in the right panel. Results update as you type.

  4. Copy or reset

    Click "Copy result" to use the output, or "Reset" to clear both panels and start fresh.

  5. Monitor size

    Check the "Size delta" stat to see how much the encoding expanded your content.

About this tool

Everything you need to know about Base64 encoding and decoding.

Base64 is a binary-to-text encoding scheme that converts binary data into ASCII characters. It's widely used for embedding files in HTML, CSS, JSON, and XML, or for safely transmitting data through text-only protocols.
