Best practices

Keep URLs readable and consistent across marketing, docs, and product sites.

  • Use hyphens for SEO-friendly slugs; underscores are treated as characters.
  • Strip numbers when release versions belong elsewhere (e.g., metadata fields).
  • Preview final URLs with stakeholders before pushing to production.

How to use

Generate URL-friendly slugs from your headlines in just a few steps.

  1. Enter your headline

    Paste or type your page title, headline, or product name in the source field.

  2. Choose separator

    Select hyphen (-), underscore (_), or plus (+) as your word separator.

  3. Configure options

    Toggle "Remove numbers" if you want to strip digits from the slug.

  4. Preview and copy

    See the generated slug and full URL preview, then copy it for use.

  5. Track history

    View your recent slugs in the sidebar for quick reuse across projects.
