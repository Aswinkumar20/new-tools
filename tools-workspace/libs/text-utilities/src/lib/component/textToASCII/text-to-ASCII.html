How to use
  • Choose From and To formats in the sidebar.
  • Paste, type, Upload, or drag & drop in Input — conversion runs automatically.
  • Use Swap or → In to reverse formats or reuse output.
  • ASCII, binary, and hex values are space-separated (e.g. 72 101 108).
