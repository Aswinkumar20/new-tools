How to use

Transform your text into any case format with our comprehensive converter.

  1. Enter your text

    Paste or type your content in the source text panel. The preview updates automatically.

  2. Select a case preset

    Choose from 20+ presets including standard, programming, and stylistic cases.

  3. Preview the result

    See the transformed output in real-time as you type or change presets.

  4. Copy or download

    Use "Copy output" to paste the result, or "Download TXT" to save it as a file.

  5. Use history

    Press Ctrl+Z (or Cmd+Z on Mac) to undo, or Ctrl+Y to redo changes.
