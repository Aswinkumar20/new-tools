How to use TopoJSON Viewer
  • Supported uploads: .topojson and .json Topology documents with objects and arcs.
  • Filter by topology object (or All), then by geometry type; click a feature to focus and inspect.
  • Scroll to zoom, drag to pan, or use Fit / Zoom controls (+ − F 0).
  • Export TopoJSON, converted GeoJSON, CSV, or summary JSON — all processed in your browser. Map tiles load from OpenStreetMap.
