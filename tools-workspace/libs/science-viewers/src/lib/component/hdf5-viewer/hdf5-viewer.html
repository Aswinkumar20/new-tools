  • Upload {{ formatsLabel }} locally, or load the built-in sample. Files never leave your browser.
  • Switch Preview, Tree, or Attributes. Filter the tree with /; Escape clears the filter or closes Export.
  • For 3D arrays, pick a plane and slice. Zoom with the wheel or +/−; f fits; i inverts.
  • Export the original file, summary/tree/dataset JSON, dataset CSV, or a PNG preview snapshot.
