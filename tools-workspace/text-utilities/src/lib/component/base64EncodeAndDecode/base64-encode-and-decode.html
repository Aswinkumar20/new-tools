Base64 Encoder & Decoder

Base64 encoding is a method of converting binary data into text format. It's commonly used for sending data over email, embedding images in web pages, and handling binary data in APIs.

Base64 decoding is the process of converting Base64-encoded text back into its original binary form. It's commonly used to retrieve files, images, or other data that were previously encoded for safe transmission or storage in text-based formats such as emails, APIs, or web pages.
